Monetization Tracking Plan

New events added to the tracking plan to support payment and revenue metrics.

Event Name Trigger Point Required Payload Properties Business Purpose
checkout_started Company clicks "Proceed to Payment" or "Subscribe" plan_id amount currency Track top-of-funnel payment intent. Used for Payment Conv. Rate.
payment_processed Payment gateway returns success callback transaction_id amount payment_method Core revenue event. Calculates Gross Revenue and ARPC.
payment_failed Payment gateway returns error/decline error_code error_message amount Monitor gateway health and payment friction points.
subscription_started Company successfully activates a recurring plan plan_tier billing_cycle Calculates Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth.
subscription_cancelled Company opts out of auto-renewal reason days_active Calculates Churn Rate and impacts Customer LTV.
refund_issued Admin processes a refund in the gateway transaction_id amount reason Calculates net revenue and Refund Rate KPI.
